# Meeting Summary

## Key Themes

### Sleep Deprivation and Leadership
- Sleep deprivation has become a status symbol, particularly among men, but it negatively impacts decision-making and effectiveness.
- The speaker argues that adequate sleep (7.5-8 hours) enables better leadership by allowing individuals to "see the iceberg before it hits the Titanic."
- Examples provided:
  - A man bragging about only 4 hours of sleep during a dinner
  - Washington professionals scheduling breakfast meetings at 8 AM while claiming it's "too late"

### Women's Role in Leadership
- Women are positioned to lead a "new revolution" in feminist issues.
- The speaker suggests that if Lehman Brothers had included more women ("sisters") who prioritized sleep, the 2008 financial crisis might have been avoided.

### Broader Implications
- Personal well-being (adequate sleep) leads to:
  - More joy and gratitude
  - Greater effectiveness
  - Better career outcomes
- These personal benefits also serve the greater good in addressing global crises.

### Smarter Food Systems (IBM Initiative)
- **Problem:** 25% of the world's food supply is lost to spoilage annually ($458 billion).
- **Solution:** Building intelligence into physical things to track food from farm to table.
- **Current implementations:** Canada, Norway, and Vietnam
- **Benefits:** 
  - Monitor travel distance and temperature
  - Ensure food arrives fresh and safe
- **IBM's mission:** "Let's build a smarter planet."

## Call to Action
- Prioritize sleep to unlock creativity and effectiveness.
- Embrace smarter systems (like IBM's food tracking) to address global challenges.
